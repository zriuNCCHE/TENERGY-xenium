## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT & THE CORE CHALLENGE
The objective is to identify shared, conserved biological Meta-Programs (MPs) 
across malignant cells from multiple ESCC patients. 

* **The Problem:** In ESCC, malignant cells have massive patient-specific Copy 
  Number Variations (CNVs). Standard clustering or matrix factorization groups cells 
  by "Patient Identity" (Clones) rather than shared functional biology (e.g., 
  Proliferation, Hypoxia, Keratinization).
* **The Baseline:** We started with the methodology from Gavish et al. (Nature 2023), 
  which used multi-K Non-negative Matrix Factorization (NMF) on pan-cancer 
  scRNA-seq data to find 41 universal meta-programs.
* **The Reality:** The Gavish pipeline failed on our data. It is built for massive 
  (1000+ patient) whole-transcriptome cohorts. Applied to a targeted 5k Xenium 
  panel for a smaller cohort, their strict filters acted like a "black hole," 
  erasing subtle but critical spatial states (like scattered MKI67+ cycling cells).

---

## 2. HOW OUR METHOD DIVERS FROM THE ORIGINAL (GAVISH ET AL.)

To adapt the pipeline for high-resolution spatial data, we made several critical 
architectural deviations:

### A. Patient-Specific Centering (The Clone Neutralizer)
* **Original:** Centered genes across all cells in a study.
* **Our Method:** We enforce strict per-patient centering (`centered_pos` layer).
* **Rationale:** We subtract the mean expression profile of *each patient* from 
  their own cells, replacing negative values with 0. This mathematically deletes 
  the CNV/Clonal background bias, forcing NMF to factorize only the intra-tumor 
  variance. This was the breakthrough that finally revealed the Proliferation MP.

### B. Normalization Strategy
* **Original:** log2(CPM/10 + 1). Designed to suppress sparse scRNA-seq noise.
* **Our Method:** Standard sc.pp.log1p. 
* **Rationale:** Xenium 5k is highly dense and sensitive. Dividing by 10 artificially 
  flattens the dynamic range of the spatial probes.

### C. Clustering & Robustness Triage
* **Original:** Aggressive 3-step filtering (must appear across multiple Ks and 
  patients with high overlap) followed by a binary "Founder-based" clustering.
* **Our Method:** Hierarchical clustering (ward/average linkage) with a relaxed 
  consensus threshold (gene must appear in >= 20% of patient-programs in a cluster).
* **Rationale:** Relaxing the strictness prevents the algorithm from deleting 
  clinically relevant "Mega-States" that are slightly noisier but highly 
  predictive of treatment resistance.

## 3. TESTS CONDUCTED & LESSONS LEARNED

### Test 1: Raw Counts vs. Centered Positives
* **Action:** Ran NMF on standard log-normalized counts.
* **Result:** Failed. Heatmaps were a "starry night" mess.
* **Lesson:** NMF cannot overcome patient CNV bias on its own. The `centered_pos` 
  transformation is non-negotiable for this cohort.

### Test 2: The "Purity vs. Clinical Significance" Trade-off
* **Action:** Attempted to "purify" a mixed resistance program (ECM + Hypoxia) by 
  pushing resolution high (K=10 to 18) and restricting genes (top_n=30, threshold=0.35).
* **Result:** The heatmap became mathematically beautiful (sharp, distinct blocks). 
  However, the clinical significance (cCR vs. non-cCR violin plots) plummeted.
* **Lesson (CRITICAL):** In ESCC, therapeutic resistance is driven by a complex 
  p-EMT "Mega-State" (e.g., LAMA3, MMP14, COL17A1 co-expressed with Hypoxia/Stress). 
  High-resolution NMF fractures this functional unit into pure, but clinically weak, 
  sub-programs. **We must prioritize lower K ranges to preserve complex biological states.**

### Test 3: Handling Sparse Matrices & NaNs
* **Action:** Attempted to extract and cluster sub-structures of specific MPs.
* **Result:** Threw `FloatingPointError: NaN dissimilarity value` and `ValueError`.
* **Lesson:** When subsetting Xenium data, some genes drop to zero variance. 
  Attempting to run `df.corr()` on zero-variance genes yields NaNs. The AI must 
  always include a zero-variance filter (`df = df.loc[:, df.std() > 0]`) before 
  clustering or correlating subsets. 

### Test 4: GPU Acceleration Bottlenecks
* **Action:** Ran the multi-K NMF grid search on a GPU-enabled HPC node.
* **Result:** GPU utilization was 0%. Scikit-learn is strictly CPU-bound.
* **Lesson:** We updated the architecture to conditionally import `cuml.decomposition.NMF` 
  (RAPIDS) if available, converting input matrices to dense `float32`. This reduces 
  compute time from hours to seconds.

---

## 4. THE OPTIMAL "TENERGY" PIPELINE PARAMETERS

For the incoming AI developing this further, do NOT try to over-engineer the 
filtering. Stick to these battle-tested parameters for the best clinical readouts:

1. **Pre-processing:** Must use the `centered_pos` layer logic.
2. **NMF K-Range:** `k_range=(7, 15)`. (Do not push to 30; it fractures the biology).
3. **Gene Definition:** `top_n=50`. (Captures the full weight of the state).
4. **MP Clusters:** `n_mps=10` to `15`.
5. **Consensus Threshold:** `0.20`. (Allows complex states to form).

## 5. KEY BIOLOGICAL ANCHORS (GROUND TRUTHS)
When refactoring or testing new code, the AI should verify the pipeline is working 
by checking for these known states:
* **Proliferation:** Should see a distinct MP dominated by `MKI67`, `TOP2A`.
* **Keratinization:** Should see a distinct MP with `KRT1`, `KRT10`, `IVL`.
* **Invasive/Resistance Front:** The holy grail of this dataset. Look for a program 
  containing `MMP14`, `COL17A1`, `LAMA3`, `LAMC2`, `CD44`, often mixed with metabolic 
  stress (`LDHA`, `SLC7A5`). This state is highly enriched in non-cCR patients.

## 6. DEVELOPMENT RULES FOR THE NEXT AI
1. **State Management:** Always clear `self.all_programs = []` at the start of the 
   `run_pipeline()` method. Failing to do so causes massive array accumulation and 
   throws `IndexError: index out of bounds` during Seaborn plotting.
2. **Matrix Handling:** Xenium data is large. Safely handle sparse matrices using 
   `.toarray()` only when necessary for NMF or correlation to avoid memory explosion.
3. **Plotting:** Rely on `sc.pl.spatial` to validate that the discovered MPs map 
   cleanly to tumor architectures (e.g., the Invasive/Resistance MP should map to 
   the stroma-tumor interface).
