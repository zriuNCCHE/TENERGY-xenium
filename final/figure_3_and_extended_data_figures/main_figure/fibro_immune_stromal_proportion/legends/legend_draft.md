# Stromal subset proportions across treatment

(A) Proportions of selected fibroblast and endothelial subsets among fibro-immune cells in cCR and non-cCR samples across pre, postC and postA timepoints. Fibro-immune cells were defined after excluding malignant and A2ML1+ epithelial cells. Points denote patient samples; grey lines connect paired samples. Boxes show median and interquartile range. P values are unadjusted paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
