# PostC iCAF proportions by clinical outcome

(A) PostC iCAF_CXCL5 and iCAF_CXCL6 proportions among fibro-immune cells in cCR and non-cCR samples. Fibro-immune cells were defined after excluding malignant and A2ML1+ epithelial cells. Points denote patient samples. Boxes show median and interquartile range. P values are unadjusted two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests.
