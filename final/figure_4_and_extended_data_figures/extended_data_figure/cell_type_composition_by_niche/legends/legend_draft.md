# Supplementary Figure 4. Cell-type composition of spatial niches

Stacked barplot showing the proportion of grouped final cell types within each post-chemoradiotherapy spatial niche.
Each cell was assigned to one spatial niche by selecting the highest neighborhood score among the seven NMF-derived spatial neighborhoods.
The y-axis shows the percentage of all cells assigned to each niche. Low-abundance cell types are grouped as Other for readability.
