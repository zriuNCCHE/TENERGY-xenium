# Exhausted CD8 T-cell proportion across treatment

(A) CD8_Tex_PDCD1 proportions among refined T cells in cCR and non-cCR samples across pre, postC and postA timepoints. Refined T cells were defined using annotated CD4 and CD8 T-cell subtypes and excluding the generic T/NK bucket. Points denote patient samples; grey lines connect paired samples. Boxes show median and interquartile range. P values are unadjusted paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
