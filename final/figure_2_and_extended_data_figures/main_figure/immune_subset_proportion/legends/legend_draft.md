# Selected immune subset proportions across treatment

(A) Proportions of selected immune subsets among fibro-immune cells in cCR and non-cCR samples across pre, postC and postA timepoints. Fibro-immune cells were defined after excluding malignant and A2ML1+ epithelial cells. Points denote patient samples; grey lines connect paired samples. Boxes show median and interquartile range. Raw P values are paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests comparing pre versus postC; adjusted P values are Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted across the displayed pre versus postC comparisons.
