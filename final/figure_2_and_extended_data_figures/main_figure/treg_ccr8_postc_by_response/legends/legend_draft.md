# CCR8+ Treg proportion after chemo-radiotherapy

(A) PostC CD4_Treg_CCR8 proportions among refined T cells in cCR and non-cCR samples. Refined T cells were defined using annotated CD4 and CD8 T-cell subtypes and excluding the generic T/NK bucket. Points denote patient samples. Boxes show median and interquartile range. P value is an unadjusted two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test.
