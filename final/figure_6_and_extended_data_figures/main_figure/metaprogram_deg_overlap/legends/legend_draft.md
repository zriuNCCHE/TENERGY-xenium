# Metaprogram and DEG overlap

(D) Heatmap summarizing overlap between malignant-cell metaprogram genes and direction-specific DEG contrasts. DEGs were defined as genes with adjusted P < 0.05 and log2 fold change >= 0.25 in the indicated direction. Counts indicate metaprogram genes that are higher in pretreatment non-cCR malignant cells, higher after chemo-radiotherapy in non-cCR malignant cells, or shared across both directions. MP7 contained the largest shared overlap, with 16 of 27 genes higher in both pretreatment non-cCR and post-chemoradiotherapy non-cCR malignant cells.

(E) Focused evidence matrix for MP7 basal/invasive matrix-remodeling genes. Color denotes log2 fold change and point size denotes adjusted P value magnitude, capped for visualization. The 16 shared MP7 genes were LAMB3, LAMC2, COL17A1, LAMA3, ITGB4, TNC, PLAU, INHBA, ITGA3, NDRG1, MYH9, SLC2A1, SERPINE1, SLC7A5, MMP14 and CDKN1A. Their log2 fold changes ranged from 0.252 to 1.205 in pretreatment non-cCR versus cCR malignant cells and from 0.353 to 1.769 in non-cCR post-chemoradiotherapy versus pretreatment malignant cells. Exact gene-level log2 fold changes and adjusted P values are saved in `results/metaprogram_deg_gene_evidence.csv`.
