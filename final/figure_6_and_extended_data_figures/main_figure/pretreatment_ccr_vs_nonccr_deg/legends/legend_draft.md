# Pretreatment malignant-cell differential expression by response

(B) Volcano plot showing differentially expressed genes in pretreatment malignant cells from non-cCR patients compared with cCR patients. Positive log2 fold change indicates higher expression in pretreatment non-cCR malignant cells. Colored points indicate genes passing adjusted P < 0.05 and absolute log2 fold change >= 0.25. Open circles mark selected extracellular-matrix and basement-membrane remodeling candidate genes.
