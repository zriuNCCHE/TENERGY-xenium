# Longitudinal malignant-cell differential expression in non-cCR patients

(C) Volcano plot showing differentially expressed genes in non-cCR malignant cells after chemo-radiotherapy compared with pretreatment non-cCR malignant cells. Positive log2 fold change indicates higher expression after chemo-radiotherapy. Colored points indicate genes passing adjusted P < 0.05 and absolute log2 fold change >= 0.25. Open circles mark selected extracellular-matrix and basement-membrane remodeling candidate genes.
